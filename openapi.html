Gemini Bridge API (0.0.0)

Download OpenAPI specification:

Gemini Bridge exposes AI generation, activity logs, authentication, health checks, and messaging platform webhook endpoints. It connects Groq/OpenAI-compatible inference with Notion logging, optional PostgreSQL persistence, Telegram, and Facebook Messenger.

Health

Service health and runtime metadata.
